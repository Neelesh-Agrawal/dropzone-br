# Dropzone

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Friends playing a browser action game together with keyboard and mouse.

## Product Purpose

A playable military battle royale with solo practice, cooperative private rooms against bots, and free-for-all private rooms against friends and bots.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing Three.js client and Node WebSocket simulation. Vercel is the intended frontend deployment. Persistent room state currently requires one long-running server process. Public hosting is not configured. Profiles and progression are local to the browser.

## Brand Commitments

Keep Dropzone and Ashen Valley. User requests PUBG / Call of Duty-inspired combat clarity, richer visuals and animation, player and kill counts, weapon and attachment visibility. Use original assets and names.
